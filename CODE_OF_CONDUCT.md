# Code of Conduct

This is a personal project. Please be respectful.

## Guidelines

- Be kind and constructive in issues and discussions
- No harassment, spam, or inappropriate content
- I reserve the right to close issues or block users who don't follow these guidelines

## Contact

For any concerns, open an issue or reach out directly.
